Best time to post

The best time is when your viewers are online — check YouTube Studio → Analytics → Audience → "When your viewers are on YouTube" once you have some views. Until then, publish about 2–3 hours before your audience's evening peak so the video is ready when they arrive:

Main audienceGood time to publish (Nigeria time, WAT)
Nigeria / West Africa3pm – 5pm on weekdays · late morning on weekends
UK / Europe3pm – 5pm

East & Southern Africa2pm – 4pm

These are starting points, not rules. Consistency — posting on the same days each week — matters more than the exact hour.

Monetization Guide

How YouTube actually pays creators — especially in Nigeria and across Africa — plus a calculator for what your views could earn.

⚠ The requirements are going up on 1 February 2027

Right now, ad revenue needs 1,000 subscribers + 4,000 watch hours in 12 months (or 10 million Shorts views in 90 days). For anyone who applies from 1 February 2027, that becomes 8,000 watch hours (or 20 million Shorts views). The 1,000-subscriber bar stays the same. If you're growing now, try to qualify and apply before that date.

Getting paid in Nigeria — step by step

  1. Create an AdSense account when you apply for the Partner Program in YouTube Studio → Earn, and verify your identity and address when asked.
  2. Submit your US tax info in AdSense → Payments → Manage tax info (form W-8BEN for individuals). Without it, Google can withhold up to 24% of all your earnings. With it, only earnings from US viewers are taxed — Nigeria has no tax treaty with the US, so that rate is usually 30%. Check the exact rate AdSense shows after you submit.
  3. Add a payment method. AdSense pays by wire transfer in US dollars once your balance passes $100, around the 21st of each month. Many Nigerian creators report failed or returned payments to local domiciliary accounts, so a lot of them use a virtual US-dollar account from a fintech app instead. Compare fees, exchange rates and reviews before choosing one.
  4. Allow time. International transfers often take several extra business days to arrive after AdSense sends them.
  5. Remember Nigerian tax. Online income is taxable in Nigeria too. Keep records, and speak to a tax professional as your income grows.

This is general information, not financial or tax advice. Payment options and rules change — always confirm in your AdSense account and YouTube Help.
